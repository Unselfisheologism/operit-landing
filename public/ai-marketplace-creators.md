# AI Marketplace for Creators — Twent

Twent is building the agentic marketplace for Android: skills, workflows, plugins, and MCP servers. Developers and creators can ship reusable AI behaviors once, earn from every install.

## Why build on Twent
- Reach Android power users searching for AI automation.
- Monetize skills and workflows without store fees.
- Distribute MCP servers and Composio integrations.

## Twent vs n8n
n8n is a powerful workflow automation platform. Twent brings that power onto Android with local AI, terminal access, and floating overlay.

## Twent vs Zapier
Zapier connects web apps. Twent connects web apps, your terminal, local models, and your Android UI.

Download Twent directly from twent.xyz. No account required.
