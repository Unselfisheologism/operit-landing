# Twent Dashboard

This is a signed-in-only page — it requires a Twent account and shows your personal billing details, so there is no public content here.

## What It Shows

When signed in, the dashboard displays:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Account email | The email on your Twent profile |
| Subscription tier | Free, Pro ($9.99/month), or Lifetime |
| Subscription started | When your current plan began |
| Subscription expires | Renewal/expiry date, if applicable |
| Billing portal link | Self-service subscription management |

## Actions Available

- View current plan and status
- Open the billing portal to update payment details or cancel
- Sign out
